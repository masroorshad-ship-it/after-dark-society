About us

Made for the after hours

After Dark Society is an event management company built on one idea: a great night out should feel exclusive, effortless and unforgettable — from the moment you RSVP to the last song.

What we do

Nights, curated end to end

We partner with the city's best clubs to produce exclusive Bollywood nights — booking the talent, designing the room, managing the guest list and looking after every table.

Beyond our own nights we run two music brands, Bolly Room and Sound Society, each with its own sound and its own crowd.
